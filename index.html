The technology

Simple sensing. Smarter packaging.

SmartFresh brings together three ideas: sustainable packaging, a natural colour indicator, and digital image analysis.

01 / PACKAGING

Sustainable by design

A packaging concept developed with sustainability and practical food monitoring in mind.

Science behind SmartFresh

Turning a colour response into data.

SmartFresh uses an anthocyanin-based colour indicator as the sensing element. The visible response can then be captured and interpreted digitally.

01
Natural indicator

Anthocyanin compounds provide the colour-changing sensing response.

02
Colour transition

The sensor response moves through the calibrated blue, pink and red reference range.

03
Image capture

The sensor can be photographed using a device camera or an uploaded image.

04
RGB comparison

The detected colour is compared with prototype reference RGB values.

For a better reading

Follow these simple steps when capturing the sensor.

01
Keep the sensor visible

Make sure the indicator is clearly inside the camera view.

02
Avoid strong reflections

Try to use even lighting and avoid direct glare.

03
Keep the camera steady

Hold the device still while capturing the image.

04
Centre the sensor

Place the sensor near the guide area for a consistent reading.
